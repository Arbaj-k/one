A personal AI assistant for everyday life

One brings everyday planning and personal information into a single workspace. Use it to review tasks, capture notes, organize projects, keep useful documents together and maintain personal money records. Ask One can help you interact with workspace information when connected AI features are available.

Open One workspace

Bring everyday information into one place

Instead of separating every note, task and project across unrelated tools, One is designed to keep everyday information close together. Start with your current priorities and return to the notes or documents that provide context.

Ask One about your workspace

Ask One is the AI-assisted interface for working with information in your One workspace. Availability and answer quality depend on the connected AI service, account configuration and network. Check important answers against the original record and confirm changes before applying them.

Use one workspace across personal and work life

One is intended for general personal organization, including work, learning, household responsibilities and personal goals. You can use the same workspace concepts to plan a day, keep project context and track routine money entries.

Understand local storage and connected services

Some information may be stored in the browser, while cloud sync and AI features depend on the services configured for your account. Do not use One as a password manager or store payment credentials and highly sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

What does a personal AI assistant do in One?

One combines task planning, notes, projects, documents and money records in a personal workspace. Ask One can assist with workspace information when AI services are enabled.

Is One only for students?

No. One is designed as a general-purpose personal organizer for everyday life, work, learning and personal goals.

Does One work without an internet connection?

Some locally stored information may remain available in the browser, but AI and cloud features require connectivity. Offline behavior depends on the feature and device.

About One: One is an evolving personal productivity app. Feature availability can depend on your browser, account, network and connected services. Review the Terms & Conditions before use.
