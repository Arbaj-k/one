Expense tracker and personal money manager

Use One's money area to keep a record of everyday spending and track informal lending or borrowing. Bring basic money records into the same personal workspace you use for tasks, notes and projects.

Open One workspace

Record everyday expenses

Keep track of spending entries so you can review where your money is going. A simple expense record can help you maintain awareness of day-to-day purchases.

Track money lent and borrowed

Record informal lending and borrowing details to help remember who owes what and which payments may need follow-up. Keep entries accurate and review them when circumstances change.

Keep money records with your plans

One brings money tracking into a broader personal organizer. It is intended for personal record keeping, not as a bank account, payment processor, tax service or regulated financial adviser.

Privacy and backups

Money records may be stored in your browser and, where cloud sync is configured, processed by connected services. Do not treat the app as a secure financial vault; keep appropriate backups and avoid storing credentials or payment card details.

Frequently asked questions

Does One include an expense tracker?

Yes. One includes a money area for recording spending and reviewing personal money entries.

Can I track money I lend to someone?

One supports informal lending and borrowing records so you can keep track of amounts and repayments.

Is One a banking or payment app?

No. One is a personal organization and record-keeping workspace, not a bank or payment processor.
