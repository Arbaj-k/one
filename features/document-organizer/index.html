A personal document organizer for everyday information

Receipts, PDFs, screenshots and reference files can become difficult to find when they are scattered. One includes a Vault area intended to keep useful documents connected to your wider personal workspace.

Open One workspace

Keep useful files with your personal workspace

Use the document area to collect files and reference material that support your plans, projects and everyday responsibilities. Availability of file types and storage depends on the current app configuration.

Turn documents into information you can revisit

Document extraction and AI-assisted workflows may help interpret supported uploads when those services are available. Always compare extracted text and generated summaries with the original file, especially for dates, names, totals and legal or financial details.

Connect documents with tasks and notes

A document is often useful because of the task or project it belongs to. One is designed to bring these parts of personal organization closer together rather than treating every file as an isolated item.

Protect sensitive information

Do not upload passwords, payment card details, identity credentials or highly sensitive third-party information. Keep separate backups of important files and review the product's terms and storage behavior.

Frequently asked questions

Can One organize PDFs and images?

One has a Vault and document workflows intended for supported files. Exact upload and extraction support can depend on the file, browser, account and connected services.

Does One automatically understand every document?

No. Extraction and AI interpretation can be incomplete or incorrect. Check important details against the original document.

Are my files automatically synced everywhere?

Cloud sync depends on account configuration, connectivity and the services enabled for your account. Keep backups of important files.
