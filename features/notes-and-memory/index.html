Notes and personal knowledge management in one workspace

One gives you a place to capture notes and ideas and keep useful information close to your tasks and projects. Build a personal reference space for everyday planning, work, learning and the things you want to remember.

Open One workspace

Capture notes and ideas

Save information you want to revisit and keep notes alongside the rest of your personal workflow. Use notes to collect thoughts, meeting details, plans and useful references.

Connect notes with projects and tasks

Keeping notes and tasks in one workspace helps preserve the context behind your plans. Return to supporting information when reviewing a project or deciding what to do next.

Find information with Ask One

Ask One can help you interact with information saved in your workspace when AI features are enabled. Always review AI-generated answers and confirm important details against your original notes.

Your personal information

Storage and sync behavior can depend on your browser, signed-in account and connected services. Review One's terms before storing important information, and keep your own backups.

Frequently asked questions

Can One be used as a personal knowledge base?

One can help keep notes and useful information together with tasks and projects in a personal workspace.

Can AI help me find my notes?

Ask One is designed to work with workspace information when its AI service is available. Results should be checked against original notes.

Are notes synced across devices?

Cloud sync depends on account configuration, connectivity and the connected services available to your account.
