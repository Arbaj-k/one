AI task manager and daily planner

Use One to organize everyday tasks, keep deadlines visible and bring your plans into one personal workspace. Manage responsibilities across work, study, personal goals and daily life. Ask One can help you work with saved workspace information when AI features are enabled.

Open One workspace

Organize tasks in one place

Keep a list of things to do and return to it as your day changes. One is designed to bring tasks together with the notes, projects and documents that provide context for your work.

Plan around deadlines

Use task due dates and your daily overview to stay aware of what is coming up. A central task list can make it easier to review priorities instead of relying on scattered reminders.

Use AI assistance with your workspace

Ask One is intended to help you find and work with information in your One workspace. AI availability and results depend on connected services, network conditions and account settings. Review suggested changes before confirming them.

Who can use this task manager?

One is a general-purpose personal organizer for everyday responsibilities, work, learning and personal projects.

Frequently asked questions

Is One a daily planner?

Yes. One combines task organization and a daily overview to help you plan responsibilities and deadlines.

Does One have AI task assistance?

Ask One provides AI-assisted interaction with workspace information when the connected AI service is available.

Can I use One for work and personal tasks?

One is designed for general personal organization, including work, learning and everyday tasks.
