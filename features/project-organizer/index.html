A personal project organizer for plans and progress

Projects often involve more than a checklist. One is designed to keep project work close to tasks, notes and useful documents, helping you return to the context behind a goal.

Open One workspace

Keep project context near your tasks

Use a project as the place to gather its related responsibilities and information. Keep notes and supporting details close to the work so you do not have to reconstruct the background every time you return.

Review priorities and deadlines

Tasks and due dates help you see what needs attention. A daily overview can help bring current responsibilities into focus while the wider project remains organized.

Use Ask One when AI is available

Ask One can help you interact with workspace information through connected AI services. Treat generated summaries and suggestions as assistance, verify details and confirm actions before changes are made.

Make project organization fit everyday life

One is a general personal workspace, not a dedicated enterprise project-management suite. It is suited to bringing personal goals, work items and everyday plans into one place.

Frequently asked questions

Can I organize personal projects in One?

Yes. One includes a Projects area intended to keep project information together with related tasks and notes.

Can projects have tasks and notes?

One is designed to connect project organization with task and note workflows. Exact behavior may evolve as the product develops.

Is One a team project-management platform?

One is currently positioned as a personal productivity workspace. Do not assume enterprise team administration or collaboration features.

About One: One is an evolving personal productivity app. Feature availability can depend on your browser, account, network and connected services. Review the Terms & Conditions before use.
