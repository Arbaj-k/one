# One launch and backlink kit

Canonical product URL: https://arbaj-k.github.io/one/

## Approved product positioning

**One-line pitch**
One is an evolving personal workspace for organizing tasks, notes, projects, documents and everyday money records, with AI assistance when connected services are available.

**Short description**
Bring everyday planning and personal information together. Use One to manage tasks and deadlines, capture notes, organize projects, keep supported documents in one workspace, and record expenses or informal lending. One is an early-stage web app; cloud sync, document processing and AI behavior depend on the configured services, account and browser.

**Long description**
One is a general-purpose personal organizer for students, professionals, freelancers and anyone who wants a single place to plan and remember everyday life. Its workspace brings together tasks, notes, projects, document workflows and personal money records. Ask One can help users work with saved information when the connected AI service is configured and available. One is an evolving MVP, not a bank, password manager, guaranteed backup service or enterprise document vault. Users should review privacy and storage limitations before adding sensitive information.

**Suggested categories**
Personal productivity; task management; note taking; project organization; personal finance tracking; AI assistant (only where the directory's category definition fits).

**Founder / maker story**
I am building One to make everyday personal organization less fragmented. Instead of keeping tasks, notes, project context and informal money records in separate places, I am experimenting with one lightweight workspace. It is an evolving product, and I am looking for honest feedback on what is useful, confusing or missing.

## Backlink quality rules

- Prefer relevant editorial mentions, genuine user reviews, useful guest tutorials and community references that naturally cite One.
- Never buy bulk links, exchange links at scale, use private blog networks, fabricate testimonials or spam comments.
- A directory listing is not guaranteed to be indexed, followed, approved or to improve rankings.
- Track each placement with its public URL, date, approval status, link attribute (follow / nofollow / sponsored) and referral traffic.
- Do not ask friends or users for fake reviews or coordinated votes.

## Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] Confirm the public app opens reliably in a fresh browser and on mobile.
- [ ] Prepare 3–5 genuine product screenshots (desktop and mobile).
- [ ] Record a short real demo showing current functionality; do not mock unavailable features.
- [ ] Publish a clear privacy / data limitations page and support contact.
- [ ] Decide whether to purchase a custom domain; do not buy one without budget approval.
- [ ] Verify signup, guest access (if offered), core task/note flows and data separation.
- [ ] Submit manually through each directory's official form and save its review status.
- [ ] After approval, verify the listing page and its actual outbound link.
