One — Your personal AI assistant and daily planner

One is a personal AI assistant and productivity workspace that helps you organize everyday life. Bring your tasks, notes, projects, documents, routines and money records together instead of spreading them across disconnected apps. Use One as a daily planner, personal organizer and place to return to the information that matters.

Plan your day and manage tasks

Keep track of what needs to get done, organize open tasks and stay aware of upcoming deadlines. Use a simple task manager for personal goals, work, study and everyday responsibilities. Ask One can help you interact with information in your workspace when its AI features are available.

Keep notes, ideas and personal knowledge together

Capture useful information, save ideas and build a personal reference space you can revisit. Keep notes alongside projects and tasks so the context behind your plans is easier to find.

Track expenses, lending and money records

Record everyday spending and keep track of money you lend or borrow. One brings simple expense tracking and informal lending records into the same workspace you use to plan your day.

Frequently asked questions

What is One?

One is a personal AI assistant and workspace for tasks, notes, projects, documents, routines and money tracking.

Can I use One as a daily planner?

Yes. You can organize tasks and deadlines in One and use its workspace to keep plans, notes and project details together.

Does One include an expense tracker?

One includes a money area for recording spending and tracking informal lending and borrowing.

Is One available on mobile?

One is a web app designed to work in modern mobile and desktop browsers. Install availability depends on the browser and device.
