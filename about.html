About One

One is a general-purpose personal AI assistant and workspace for everyone—not just students or college communities. It brings everyday planning, personal knowledge, notes, tasks, projects, documents, and money tracking together in one place.

Use One to stay organized across daily life, work, learning, and personal goals. Learning and study tools are available when they are useful, but they are only one part of the experience.

One is designed to keep your workspace local-first, with account and cloud features available as they are configured. The current version is an evolving MVP; features and availability may change.

Your information

Some information is saved in your browser. If cloud or AI features are enabled, data may also be processed through connected services. Avoid uploading confidential, highly sensitive, or third-party personal information.
