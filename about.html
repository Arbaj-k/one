About Student Brain

Student Brain is a personal workspace designed to help students organize study plans, tasks, projects, expenses, and useful knowledge in one place.

The current version is an MVP preview. Features and availability may change as the project develops.

Your information

Some information may be saved in your browser. If cloud features are enabled, data may also be processed through connected services. Avoid uploading confidential, highly sensitive, or third-party personal information.
