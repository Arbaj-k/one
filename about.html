About One — Your personal AI assistant

One is a general-purpose personal AI assistant and workspace for everyone—not just students or college communities. It brings everyday planning, personal knowledge, notes, tasks, projects, documents, and money tracking together in one place.

Use One to stay organized across daily life, work, learning, and personal goals. Learning and study tools are available when they are useful, but they are only one part of the experience.

One is designed to keep your workspace local-first, with account and cloud features available as they are configured. The current version is an evolving MVP; features and availability may change.

What can you organize with One?

  • Tasks and daily planning — capture responsibilities, review deadlines and keep next actions visible.
  • Notes and personal knowledge — save ideas, reference details and information you want to revisit.
  • Personal projects — keep related work, notes and tasks together.
  • Documents and files — organize supported PDFs, images and reference material in the Vault.
  • Expenses and money owed — maintain personal spending, lending and borrowing records.
  • Ask One and AI assistance — interact with selected workspace information when AI services are enabled.

How does Ask One work?

Ask One is an AI-assisted way to interact with information saved in your workspace. What it can retrieve depends on the records and documents available to your account, the features enabled and the connected AI service. AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect, so check important details against the original record and confirm changes before applying them.

Is One suitable for work and personal life?

Yes. One is designed as a general-purpose personal organizer for everyday responsibilities, work, learning and personal goals. It is not an enterprise team-management platform, bank, payment processor or professional financial adviser.

Frequently asked questions

What is One?

One is a personal productivity workspace that combines task planning, notes, projects, document organization and personal money records.

Is One only for students?

No. One is designed for anyone who wants a single place to organize everyday life, work, learning and personal goals.

Does One work offline?

Some locally stored information may remain available in your browser. AI and cloud features require an internet connection, and offline behavior varies by feature and device.

Does One sync across devices?

Cloud sync depends on your account, connectivity and the services configured for your version of One. Check the connection status in the app and keep independent backups of important information.

Is One a bank or payment app?

No. Its Money area is for personal record keeping, such as spending and informal lending or borrowing. It does not process payments or provide regulated financial advice.

Your information

Some information is saved in your browser. If cloud or AI features are enabled, data may also be processed through connected services. Avoid uploading confidential, highly sensitive, or third-party personal information.
