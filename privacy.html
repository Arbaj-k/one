Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This page explains how the One personal productivity workspace may handle information when you use the website and app. One is an evolving project; actual features and data flows depend on the version deployed, your account settings, browser and connected services. This policy describes the current intended app behavior and known integrations, not a guarantee about third-party provider retention.

Important: Do not store passwords, payment card details, identity credentials, confidential business information or highly sensitive personal information in One. Keep independent backups of important records.

1. Information you provide

  • Account information: email address, authentication details and optional profile information when you sign up or use a supported sign-in provider.
  • Workspace information: tasks, notes, projects, personal memories, reminders, chat messages and money records you choose to save.
  • Files: uploaded documents, file names and extracted text when you use supported Vault and document features.
  • Optional companion features: if you enable Android SMS sync or notifications, the companion or notification feature may process the limited transaction fields or device subscription information described below.

2. Where information may be stored

Some workspace information is stored in your browser using local browser storage so that local-first and offline features can work. Depending on the feature and your account configuration, workspace records may also be synced to Supabase. Uploaded files may be held in the browser's IndexedDB cache and/or the private One document storage bucket. Removing browser data can remove local copies; do not assume that clearing browser data deletes cloud copies.

4. AI features

When you use Ask One or a document AI workflow, the app may send your prompt and selected context to the configured server-side AI function, which can forward it to Google Gemini. Only use AI features with information you are comfortable sharing with the connected provider. AI-generated output can be incomplete or wrong; verify important details against your original records before relying on them or making changes.

5. Analytics and diagnostics

The app includes first-party usage analytics intended to record events such as page or feature usage with a pseudonymous browser identifier. The app analytics implementation is designed not to include the contents of your notes, chats or money records in event payloads. The public website also uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-HELE44ZGB0) to measure page views and general website usage. Google may process page URL, referrer and browser/device signals under its own terms and settings; cookies or similar identifiers may be used depending on browser settings. Hosting and connected service providers may separately process operational logs, such as IP address, device or request information. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings. Analytics behavior may change as the project evolves; consult this page for updates.

6. Optional Android SMS sync and notifications

If you separately install and enable the Android companion's SMS sync feature, it may parse transaction-related fields such as amount, direction, merchant, UPI reference, sender and timestamp and send those parsed fields to the configured sync endpoint. The inspected app flow is designed to send parsed fields rather than the full SMS body; the separately deployed sync service should be reviewed independently. If you enable push notifications, task titles, dates or related titles may appear in notification text on your device, including on a lock screen depending on device settings.

7. Security and account separation

Cloud workspace tables and private document storage are designed with owner-scoped access policies. Browser-local data is accessible to scripts running in the same browser origin, and the app's browser session is not an HttpOnly-cookie-only architecture. No web app can promise absolute security. Keep your browser and device protected and sign out on shared devices.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Use app settings and account controls to review available data and privacy options.
  • Export or back up important information where the app provides that option.
  • Use the account deletion control if available in your deployed version. Deletion behavior can depend on connected services and may not remove files or copies already exported or processed by third parties.
  • Clear local browser data only when you understand that doing so may remove local-only information.

For questions or privacy requests, use the One project repository to contact the project maintainer. Do not post private account details or sensitive records in public issues.

9. Children and age

One is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the project maintainer through the repository.

10. Policy changes

This policy may change as One's features and integrations change. The date above indicates the latest revision. Check this page periodically, especially before enabling new integrations.
