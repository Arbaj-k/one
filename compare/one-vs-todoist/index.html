One vs Todoist

The main distinction is breadth versus focus: One aims to bring several personal record types together, while Todoist is centered on helping people capture and manage tasks.

AreaOneTodoist
Primary useEveryday personal workspaceDedicated task and project management
TasksTasks, due dates and completionCore product focus with mature task workflows
Notes and knowledgePersonal notes and saved informationTask/project context; not a general knowledge-base replacement
ProjectsBasic project organizationProjects, sections and task organization
Personal money recordsExpense and informal lending recordsNot its primary purpose
MaturityEvolving MVP; verify sync and AI behaviorEstablished specialist task product

Choose One if

You want to experiment with tasks alongside notes, projects, documents and simple money records in one personal workspace.

Choose Todoist if

Task capture, recurring tasks, reminders, filters and a mature dedicated task workflow are your top priorities.
